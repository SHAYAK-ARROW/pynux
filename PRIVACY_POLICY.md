# Privacy Policy for Pynux Arrow

**Last Updated:** August 20, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to Pynux Arrow. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles data and permissions on your device.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
Pynux Arrow is a local development environment. **We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data with external servers.** All code execution and data processing happen entirely offline on your device.

## 3. Permissions
Pynux Arrow may request permissions like Camera, GPS, or Bluetooth only when your Python scripts explicitly call them via the Bridge module. These are accessed locally and never used for tracking.
